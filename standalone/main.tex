\begin{abstract}
\itshape
On a polyhedral target, one finite set of points locates the maximum of every
monotone max--min combination of functions that share a convex part and differ
by affine terms, whatever the rule and the shared part; a maximiser on an
$f$-dimensional face is fixed by $f+1$ of the functions. For sensing disks this
gives exact rational coverage radii and power thresholds for every monotone
failure rule, the whole radius--budget frontier, a canonical certificate of
minimal contact sets with witnesses, exact robust tests under independent
placement errors and dual certificates for protection.
\end{abstract}
