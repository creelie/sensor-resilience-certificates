# Novelty and scope

The strongest proposed result is the universal support theorem for a shared convex part with affine differences. A finite support set works simultaneously for every monotone rule, every common convex term and every level. The fixed-dimensional support bound and its geometric consequences are proved directly in the manuscript.

The exact power–budget staircase and the fixed-radius antichain make the construction reusable across failure costs, domain maps and selected subnetworks. The product-uncertainty theorem states precisely when guaranteed footprints give a necessary-and-sufficient robust test, and includes a shared-error counterexample.

Arrangements, Voronoi/power geometry, lattice polynomials, minimal cuts, nerve theory, cover decomposition and linear-programming duality are established subjects, not discoveries claimed here. The bibliography credits them. No existing named open conjecture is claimed to be resolved. A literature search is not proof that no equivalent theorem was previously published. The word “breakthrough” and a guarantee of Annals acceptance are therefore not presented as verified facts.

The main manuscript is analytical. Its examples are exact symbolic constructions. Optional software provides supplementary finite controls and is not a premise of a proof. No Lean formalization is claimed.
